# CEStudy Foundation Resource Map

**Researched:** 2026-10-18  
**Status:** Source map checked for scope, licensing, and curriculum distinctions.  
**Purpose:** Guide the original Math Foundations pathway without copying textbook prose, figures, or question banks.

## Decision summary

1. Plan **Algebra Readiness + College Algebra together as one CEStudy pathway, provisionally named Algebra Foundations**. Begin with a readiness diagnostic and targeted review, then proceed to College Algebra. Keep Trigonometry, Analytic Geometry, and Solid Mensuration as separate subjects. These pathway names are CEStudy design decisions, not course titles universally required by the current CHED BSCE program standard.
2. The official CHED BSCE standard is **CMO No. 92, s. 2017**. Its current minimum math block lists Calculus 1, Calculus 2, Differential Equations, Engineering Data Analysis, and Numerical Solutions to CE Problems (15 math units as stated by CHED). It does not list College Algebra, Advanced Algebra, Plane and Spherical Trigonometry, Analytic Geometry, or Solid Mensuration as separate minimum math courses.
3. An older CHED sample curriculum does list those five foundations as separate courses. Treat this as a historical/sample pathway, not the current national course-title mandate.
4. University curricula differ. Eastern Visayas State University (Tanauan Campus) has a published BSCE checklist effective SY 2021-2022 listing College Algebra, Plane and Spherical Trigonometry, Analytic Geometry, and Solid Mensuration before Differential Calculus. PUP's official BSCE page labels its displayed curriculum 2022-2023 and describes a bridging program for non-STEM entrants with Basic Calculus and General Physics; this shows that bridging arrangements and packaging differ.
5. For Solid Mensuration, no single permissively licensed resource was verified that comprehensively covers standard solid formulas, frustums, Cavalieri's principle, and Pappus-Guldinus theorems in one current textbook. Use a **source bundle**: CC BY 4.0 material for basic surface-area/volume foundations, with explicitly marked verification-only references for the gaps.
6. Research and citation do not mean permission to republish. CEStudy's lessons, diagrams, worked examples, and questions will be original. No source illustrations or question wording will be copied into the content pack.

## B. Selected research and verification sources

### Algebra readiness, college algebra, and trigonometry

**1. Precalculus: An Investigation of Functions (2nd edition)**  
Authors: David Lippman and Melonie Rasmussen.  
Source: [LibreCommons textbook record](https://commons.libretexts.org/book/math-13825) · [Open Textbook Library record](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/97)  
License: **CC BY-SA 4.0** on the LibreCommons record. The book covers functions, linear functions, polynomial/rational functions, exponential/logarithmic functions, trigonometric functions, trigonometric equations/identities, applications of trigonometry, and conics.  
Use: Main structure/reference for algebraic functions, lines, trigonometry, identities, applications, and conics. We are not copying its wording, worked examples, diagrams, or exercises. If a specific passage, figure, or other content is ever reused, the exact license and attribution/share-alike obligations must be reviewed first.

**2. Precalculus 2e, Chapter 10: Analytic Geometry (LibreTexts-hosted chapter)**  
Source: [Chapter 10: Analytic Geometry](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Precalculus/Precalculus_2e_(OpenStax)/10%3A_Analytic_Geometry)  
Displayed license: **CC BY 4.0** on this specific LibreTexts chapter. Last-modified date displayed by the page: 2023-05-28.  
Coverage: Conic sections, circles as a conic, ellipses, hyperbolas, parabolas, rotation of axes, and conics in polar coordinates.  
Use: Cross-check standard forms and terminology for the conic-sections portion. We will make original diagrams and explanations. Check each individual asset's attribution/license separately before any reuse.

### Solid mensuration: surface area, volume, frustums, and theorem extensions

**3. Contemporary Mathematics, Section 10.7: Volume and Surface Area (LibreTexts-hosted chapter)**  
Source: [Volume and Surface Area](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Applied_Mathematics/Contemporary_Mathematics_(OpenStax)/10%3A__Geometry/10.07%3A_Volume_and_Surface_Area)  
Displayed license: **CC BY 4.0** on this specific page. Last-modified date displayed by the page: 2025-01-02.  
Coverage: Standard surface area and volume for right prisms and cylinders, with examples and applied questions.  
Use: Primary permissively licensed reference for dimensional units, prism surface-area/volume relationships, cylinder surface area and volume. **Scope gap:** this page alone is not a complete solid mensuration text; it does not fully cover frustums, sphere zones/segments, or Pappus-Guldinus theorems.

**4. Street-Fighting Mathematics, Section 2.3: Solid Geometry - The Volume of a Truncated Pyramid (LibreTexts-hosted chapter)**  
Source: [Volume of a Truncated Pyramid](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Applied_Mathematics/Street-Fighting_Mathematics%3A_The_Art_of_Educated_Guessing_and_Opportunistic_Problem_Solving_(Mahajan)/02%3A_Easy_Cases/2.03%3A_Solid_Geometry_-_The_Volume_of_a_Truncated_Pyramid)  
Displayed license: **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** on this page.  
Coverage: Dimensional reasoning and the volume of a frustum/truncated pyramid, with an extension asking about a truncated cone.  
Use: Verification-only reference for checking dimensional reasoning and the class of frustum problem. Since the license is NonCommercial-ShareAlike, do not copy or adapt its explanatory text, diagrams, or problems into CEStudy. The frustum formulas in CEStudy will be independently written and calculated.

**5. Mathalino - Frustums (Solid Geometry Review)**  
Source: [Frustums](https://mathalino.com/reviewer/solid-mensuration-solid-geometry/frustum)  
License: No clear reuse license was established from the source page during this review.  
Use: Verification-only reference to compare common engineering-math notation and formula conventions. No text, drawing, or question from this page is to be republished.

**6. The Theorems of Pappus (Physics LibreTexts)**  
Source: [The Theorems of Pappus](https://phys.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Classical_Mechanics/Classical_Mechanics_(Tatum)/01:_Centers_of_Mass/1.06:_The_Theorems_of_Pappus)  
Displayed license: **CC BY-NC 4.0** on this page.  
Coverage: Pappus centroid theorems and using the centroid's path to relate plane area/curve length to surface area/volume of revolution.  
Use: Verification-only reference for correct theorem statements and conditions. Do not reuse its prose or figures.

**7. Pappus Centroid Theorems (University of California, Riverside mathematics handout)**  
Source: [Pappus Centroid Theorems PDF](https://math.ucr.edu/~res/math153/history05e.pdf)  
License: No reuse license was identified in the retrieved document metadata.  
Use: Secondary mathematical verification only. Do not reproduce the handout or its illustrations.

**8. Solid Mensuration syllabus example (Divine Word College of Legazpi, Summer SY 2013)**  
Source: [Course syllabus PDF](https://engrbaguiao.weebly.com/uploads/8/3/3/7/8337118/solid_mensuration_syllabus_by_baguiao.pdf)  
Date/scope: Explicitly dated Summer SY 2013, so it is a historical university syllabus, not current CHED policy. It lists lines/planes in space, Cavalieri's theorem, a volume theorem, prisms/cylinders, pyramids/cones, regular pyramid and right-circular-cone frustums, spheres/zones/segments/sectors, and Pappus theorems.  
License: No open reuse license was identified.  
Use: Topic checklist only. It shows that Cavalieri and Pappus have appeared in a Philippine Solid Mensuration course outline, but it does **not** establish them as universally required by current CHED or all current universities. CEStudy should teach their intuitive meaning as an optional/advanced extension unless a target school's current syllabus makes them required.

## D. Source and licensing policy for authors

- The default mode is **research and verification only**. A source's free-to-read status does not mean CEStudy can reproduce its content.
- Do not copy textbook prose, worked examples, figures, exercise wording, answer keys, or distinctive layouts into CEStudy.
- Create explanations, drawings, data, problem contexts, question wording, solution steps, and answer keys from scratch.
- Record each claim's sources, especially where a theorem has conditions or where conventions differ.
- If material is deliberately reused, record the exact source and version, asset-level rights where relevant, required attribution, license terms, modifications, and whether share-alike obligations apply. Do not reuse NC-licensed material in a way that conflicts with CEStudy's intended use.
- A human academic reviewer must check every solution, unit, sign, diagram, and answer key before publication. Draft question text and answer keys must stay out of the public repository, public seed data, public logs, and build artifacts. Follow [unpublished question authoring and publication](../content-authoring-and-publication.md).

## E. Original lesson-pack build order

1. **Algebra Foundations (readiness + College Algebra):** begin with a diagnostic and targeted prerequisite review, then teach real-number arithmetic, expressions and formula rearrangement, exponents/radicals/scientific notation, linear equations and inequalities, functions and graphs, polynomials and factoring, quadratics with introductory complex numbers, selected higher-degree polynomial equations, rational/radical equations with domain checks, systems, exponential/logarithmic equations, and integrated applications.
2. Trigonometry: angles, triangle ratios, unit circle, identities, triangle-solving laws, and applied height/distance problems.
3. Analytic Geometry: lines and coordinate tools before circle and conic forms.
4. Solid Mensuration: plane-area prerequisites, basic solids, surface areas/volumes, frustums, then optional theorem extensions.

The lesson pack authored in this research session is an original draft for maintainer review. It is intentionally not committed to this public repository because CEStudy's content protection policy keeps unpublished lesson/question text and answer keys out of public Git history.
