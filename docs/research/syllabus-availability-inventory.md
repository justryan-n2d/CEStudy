# CEStudy Foundation Subject Syllabus Availability Inventory

**Checked:** 2026-10-18  
**Status:** Research inventory only. No syllabus has been drafted or represented as official. For CEStudy planning, Algebra Readiness and College Algebra are grouped into one pathway provisionally named **Algebra Foundations**; the source evidence for them remains distinguished below.  
**Scope:** Public CHED documents and Philippine university curriculum/course-description pages.

## How to read this inventory

- **Syllabus found:** A document explicitly presented as a course syllabus with a topic outline was located.
- **Course description found:** An institution describes the course and lists some topics, but a full syllabus was not located.
- **Curriculum listing found:** The course appears in a program of study with units or prerequisites; that alone is not a syllabus.
- **Not found in this search:** No matching public, standalone syllabus was located. This does not prove none exists privately or elsewhere.

## Main source records

### CHED

1. [CHED CMO No. 92, Series of 2017: BS Civil Engineering program standards and guidelines](https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/CMO-92-s.-2017-BS-Civil-Engineering.pdf). This is the later BSCE program standard identified in this review, effective AY 2018-2019. Use it for current national program context, not the older foundation-course list.
2. [CHED CMO No. 29, Series of 2007: BSCE policies and standards](https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/CMO-No.29-s2007.pdf). This historical curriculum lists College Algebra, Advanced Algebra, Plane and Spherical Trigonometry, Analytic Geometry, and Solid Mensuration with minimum units and prerequisite information. The accessible 15-page copy refers to detailed course specifications in Annex III, but that annex's full subject outlines were not present in the retrieved copy. Therefore this record proves historical course/curriculum specifications, not that a complete current syllabus was obtained.

### Philippine university sources

3. [De La Salle University, Civil Engineering course descriptions](https://old.dlsu.edu.ph/colleges/gcoe/academic-departments/civil-engineering/course-description/). Detailed course descriptions and topic lists were found for College Algebra, Advanced Algebra, Plane and Spherical Trigonometry, Analytic Geometry, and Solid Mensuration. The page is not a dated, week-by-week syllabus, so it is treated as a course-description source.
4. [University of the Assumption, BS Civil Engineering curriculum PDF](https://www.ua.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/BS-Civil-Engineering-curr.pdf). A program-of-study listing places College Algebra, Plane and Spherical Trigonometry, Advanced Algebra, Analytic Geometry, and Solid Mensuration in the curriculum. The document is a curriculum listing, not a syllabus.
5. [Samar State University, BS Civil Engineering curriculum](https://ssu.edu.ph/college-of-engineering-bsce/). The published bridging-program entry lists PREP College Algebra for non-STEM entrants. It does not provide a separate syllabus titled Algebra Readiness.
6. [Historical Solid Mensuration syllabus copy, Summer SY 2013](https://engrbaguiao.weebly.com/uploads/8/3/3/7/8337118/solid_mensuration_syllabus_by_baguiao.pdf). The syllabus itself is dated 2013, but the copy was located on a third-party host. Use it as historical evidence for topics that have appeared in one Philippine course, not as current CHED policy or a license to reproduce the document.

## Conclusion

- **External-source result:** no standalone public Algebra Readiness syllabus was found; a bridging College Algebra entry and College Algebra course descriptions/curriculum listings were found. These sources did not provide a complete current syllabus for CEStudy's combined pathway. CEStudy has now drafted its own separate [Algebra Foundations syllabus](../curriculum/algebra-foundations-syllabus.md) from the approved scope. This is an original CEStudy draft, not an official university or CHED syllabus.
- **For Advanced Algebra, Plane and Spherical Trigonometry, and Analytic Geometry, course descriptions and/or older curriculum listings were found, but a full current BSCE syllabus was not verified.**
- **For Solid Mensuration, a detailed course description and a historical 2013 syllabus copy were found, but no current official complete syllabus was verified.**
- This is a record of what surfaced in public sources during this search, not proof that other universities do not have syllabi that are unpublished or not indexed.
- Keep Advanced Algebra, Trigonometry, Analytic Geometry, and Solid Mensuration separate, each with its own syllabus. Algebra Readiness and College Algebra are grouped into the single CEStudy subject Algebra Foundations, with readiness/review first and College Algebra next. The scope has been approved and an original CEStudy syllabus draft is available for academic review.
